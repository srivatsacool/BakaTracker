# 📖 User Guide

This guide walks you through using BakaTracker to log habits, manage quests, reflect on your days, and progress your RPG character profile.

---

## ⏱️ The 30-Second Daily Routine

BakaTracker is designed to prevent "productivity overwhelm". The ideal usage pattern follows this minimalist checklist:

1. **Morning Check-In (10 seconds):**
   * Open BakaTracker.
   * Read the random motivational Quote of the Day on your **Habits** board.
   * Star 1–3 tasks from your **Tasks** backlog to pin them as today's active quests.
2. **Evening Check-Out (20 seconds):**
   * Open the app.
   * Check off completed habits (e.g. Gym, Medicine) and increment counters (e.g. Water, Reading pages).
   * Drag your active quests to the "Done" column on your **Today** board.
   * Navigate to the **Journal** page, type a single sentence summarizing your day's main Highlight, rate your mood, and click **Save**.
   * Close the app.

## 🗓️ Logging Habits

The **Habits** page is your default dashboard. It lists all active trackers configured for the current day.

### Habit Types and Interfaces
* **Checkbox Habits:** Simple toggles (e.g. Brushing teeth, Gym session). Tapping the card toggles completion and updates your daily score.
* **Counter Habits:** Incremental tallies (e.g. Water intake, pages read). Use the `[-]` and `[+]` buttons to log values. XP is awarded per increment (e.g. +2 XP per page read).
* **Mood Logs:** Quick check-ins represented by three face emojis: `😞` (Low/Sad), `😐` (Neutral), and `🙂` (Happy/Great).
* **Energy Ratings:** Logs physical/mental energy states: `Low`, `Medium`, or `High`.
* **Numeric Inputs:** Logs precise floats (e.g. Sleep hours, screen time) using custom increase/decrease buttons.

### Changing Calendar Views
Use the tab toggle at the top of the Habits board:
* **Today:** The standard check-in list for the current date.
* **Month View:** Displays a physical grid layout where rows represent habits and columns represent days. Completed habits display solid color cells, making streaks and gaps instantly scannable.

## 🎯 Task Management (Kanban Boards)

BakaTracker separates task planning from daily execution to reduce cognitive overwhelm.

### The Master Board (Planning Mode)
Navigate to the **Tasks** page to review the complete life backlog. Tasks are grouped in a 4-column Kanban layout:
1. **Backlog:** Ideas, future plans, and raw brainstorm dumps.
2. **Todo:** Quests scheduled for near-term action.
3. **Doing:** Active multi-day projects.
4. **Done:** Completed life events.

To reschedule or reorganize tasks, click on a card to edit details (Title, Area category, Due Date, XP Reward, Notes) or drag cards across columns.

### The Today Board (Execution Mode)
Navigate to the **Today** page. This is a focused 3-column board showing only tasks you marked with the `Today` star:
* **Today:** Your immediate target list for the day.
* **Doing:** The single quest you are actively focusing on *right now*.
* **Done:** Completed tasks that have granted their XP rewards.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To maintain execution focus, avoid keeping more than 3 active tasks on your Today board at any given time.

## ✍️ Reflections (Journaling)

The **Journal** page acts as a memory drawer. It enforces a strict "Highlight-First" constraint.

* **Highlight of the Day (Required):** A single-sentence summary of the day (e.g., *Passed my driving test* or *Had lunch with Mom*).
* **Notes (Optional):** Space to capture secondary details, worries, or thoughts.
* **Mood & Energy Check-ins:** Auto-binds your habit logs into your journal timeline.
* **Quote Link:** BakaTracker automatically saves the quote displayed on your Habits page alongside that day's journal entry, creating a historical bookmark of your headspace.

---

## 🛡️ RPG Progression (Journey & Stats)

The **Journey** page displays your character sheet and consistency analytics.

* **Character Sheet:** Displays your overall level, total XP, and progression across five life stats (Discipline, Health, Knowledge, Creativity, and Career).
* **Github-Style Heatmap:** A visual block grid showing daily consistency scores (0 to 100). Deeper colors represent days with high habit completions, task check-offs, and logged journal entries.
* **Trends & Insights:** Scroll down to view line charts mapping Sleep trends, Screen Time averages, Mood levels, and XP growth curves. Rule-based insights summarize core patterns (e.g. *You tend to feel better on days with 8+ hours of sleep*).
